One satirical sentence (202 characters) roasting lawmakers who regulate AI, crypto and the internet but cannot convert a file to PDF, for make.fun's "The Long Roast: Lawmakers and the File They Cannot Convert" bounty ($2, top 10 split the reward).

## The sentence

> He chairs the committee on artificial intelligence, signs bills that decide what your chatbot may say, and still emails his assistant a screenshot of a Word document whenever someone asks him for a PDF.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`). One sentence, no trailing newline, 202 characters — inside the 320-character gate.
